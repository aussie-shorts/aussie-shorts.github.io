Aussie Shorts

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels

What Aussie Shorts is

Aussie Shorts is a small desktop tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish short, factual, Australian-made videos to their own YouTube channels. It isn't offered to the public, and only the owner signs in to it.

What it does with YouTube

Aussie Shorts uses the YouTube API Services. With the owner's permission it:

  • uploads finished videos to the owner's channels, with their title, description, tags and privacy setting;
  • files each video into a playlist on the same channel;
  • updates the channel's description, keywords and banner image;
  • reads back the channel's name and a video's privacy status, to confirm each upload went to the right place.

Everything it handles stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. See the privacy policy for the details.
