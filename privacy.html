Aussie Shorts

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains what Aussie Shorts does with information from Google accounts. Aussie Shorts is a desktop tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish videos to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public. You agree to this policy before signing in to Aussie Shorts.

YouTube API Services

Aussie Shorts uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What information Aussie Shorts accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant access, Aussie Shorts can:

  • read your YouTube channel's ID and name, to confirm it is connected to the right channel;
  • upload videos to your channel and set their title, description, tags, category, privacy setting and altered or synthetic content disclosure;
  • create playlists on your channel and add your videos to them;
  • update your channel's description, keywords, country and banner image;
  • read the privacy status of videos it uploaded, and delete a test video it uploaded.

It doesn't read your email, contacts, comments, subscribers, watch history or analytics.

What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in tokens. After you sign in, Google issues an access token. Aussie Shorts saves it in a file on the computer where it runs, so it can keep publishing without asking you to sign in every day. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • Video records. For each video it keeps the file, its title and description, and the YouTube video ID on the same computer.
  • There are no Aussie Shorts servers or databases. The tool and this website don't use cookies, analytics or advertising.

How information is used and shared

Information from your Google account is used only to publish and organise videos on your own channels, as listed above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not sent to any other service. No third parties serve content or advertisements through Aussie Shorts.

Aussie Shorts' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke Aussie Shorts' access at any time on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Delete the sign-in token files (in each channel's brand folder) to remove the stored access from the computer.
  • To ask for anything else to be deleted, email the contact below.
